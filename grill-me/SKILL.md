# Grill the plan

Read the supplied plan and identify the assumption whose failure would most change the decision. Ask one concrete question at a time; use each answer to challenge evidence, trade-offs or failure handling, not to repeat a fixed questionnaire.

Stay focused on the user's decision. Do not turn brainstorming into an implementation or demand unrelated background. Stop when the critical assumptions are resolved, the remaining uncertainties cannot be answered with current evidence, or the user asks to stop. Close with the decision, remaining risks and the smallest useful next check.
