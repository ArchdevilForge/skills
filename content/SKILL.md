# Website copy

Deliver the requested copy, not a full marketing strategy unless asked.

## Context

Use existing product context if available: `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `.claude/product-marketing.md` or `product-marketing-context.md`. Read only the relevant material. For a local edit, infer audience and voice from the page; ask only for missing facts that change the promise, audience or action.

For a full page, identify who it serves, their problem, the offer, available evidence and the primary action. Do not block a small headline/CTA change on a full questionnaire.

## Write

- Make the offer and next action clear. Translate features into concrete benefits without inventing savings, testimonials or results.
- Match customer language and brand voice; avoid vague superlatives and claims the product cannot support.
- Give each section a purpose. Structure and length depend on the page, not a fixed sequence of pain, proof, FAQ and CTA.
- Use a CTA that accurately names the action. Existing product terminology takes priority over generic lists of “strong” or “weak” verbs.
- Preserve legal copy, prices, existing promises and user-supplied facts unless changing them is requested.

## References by need

- Full page structure or headline alternatives: [copy frameworks](references/copy-frameworks.md).
- Awkward transitions: [transition examples](references/natural-transitions.md).
- Generic/AI-sounding phrasing: [humanizer](references/humanizer.md), as editing suggestions rather than a ban on words or punctuation.
- Frontend layout: use `design`.

## Output

Copy first, in the requested format. Add alternatives, annotations or SEO metadata only when requested or materially useful. Verify facts, consistency and the intended action; mark missing proof rather than fabricating it.
